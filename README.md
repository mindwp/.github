# MindWP

### We build the system that brings your practice its customers, and keep it working every month.

Most service practices don't lose revenue because of lack of interest—they lose it in the gaps between the first inquiry and the booked appointment:
- The phone call that goes to voicemail during busy clinic hours
- The website contact form that waits a day for a reply
- The consultation quote that is never followed up
- The satisfied patient who is never asked for a Google review

**[MindWP](https://mindwp.com)** fixes those gaps first. Then, we bring more people in with local search and targeted acquisition.

### One Team for the Whole Path

Instead of hiring one person for a website, an agency for SEO, and another tool for scheduling, MindWP manages the entire customer journey under one roof:

- **The Website:** Designed to answer patient questions quickly and make booking effortless.
- **The Follow-Up:** Instant response workflows that capture inquiries the moment they come in.
- **Local Search & Reviews:** Helping your practice rank where patients search and building authentic 5-star reputation.
- **Clear Results:** One team, one monthly report measured in actual booked customers and what they are worth to your business.
